# Threshold calibration: what the N=10 logs say

Offline analysis of the 20 official seeds of records 004 and 005 (no GPU
needed; regenerate the tables with
`python scripts/analyze_threshold.py --md docs/threshold_calibration.md`).
The threshold is **not** being changed by this document; it records the
evidence for a future calibration.

## Takeaways

1. **The spread is learning speed, not hardware.** Time = steps × s/step, and
   steps-to-threshold carries 97 % of the variance in both records; inclusive
   s/step varies by only 6 % across seeds and hosts (Modal vs RunPod). So the
   004 provider gap is a steps gap, consistent with the RULES discussion.
2. **Eval sampling noise is a real but minority component at 10 %.** The
   Wilson 95 % half-width on 2,000 puzzles at p = 0.10 is ±1.4 pp. Over the
   last eval interval before crossing the curve climbs a median 3.7 pp / 100
   steps (004) and 7.4 pp / 100 steps (005), so that ±1.4 pp is worth
   ±103 s (004) / ±29 s (005) of crossing time — up to ±200 s for the
   shallowest seeds — against observed seed sds of 348 s / 196 s. Roughly
   10 % (004) and 2 % (005) of the time variance is the eval itself.
3. **Individual crossings are statistically ambiguous.** 18 of 20 crossing
   evals have a Wilson interval that straddles 0.10 (mean overshoot 0.6–0.75
   pp). The benchmark's protection against this is the multi-seed Welch test,
   not the per-seed crossing; a single-seed time should never be quoted.
4. **The eval cadence quantises the clock.** With `eval_every = 25` a
   crossing is only observed every ~65 s (004) / ~39 s (005), adding
   U(0, one interval) to every seed's time. Recipes can trade this off
   (denser evals cost eval time on the clock); this is already part of the
   recipe by design.
5. **10 % sits in the shallow part of the curve.** The first eval at step 150
   already clears 5–7 % for every seed (the curriculum does that), and from 8 %
   to 10 % the CV of time-to-threshold rises from 0.33 to 0.43 (005) while
   mean steps go 208 → 272 → 320: ~50–60 steps per percentage point and
   slowing. 004 seeds 5 and 6 sat at a byte-identical eval pass rate for 4–5
   consecutive evals (75–100 steps) before moving again — genuine stalls
   under greedy decoding, not noise.
6. **What would make a better threshold.** The right target is a fixed
   fraction of the 0.5B plateau on 4-number Countdown, which nobody has
   measured: that needs one ~2,000-step run per recipe (~1 GPU-hour) with the
   threshold disabled. Until then 10 % stays; a higher threshold should lower
   the relative eval-noise contribution (steeper curve once past the
   curriculum hand-off, larger absolute times) but will lengthen runs.

Question: at the current 10 % threshold, how much of the seed-to-seed spread in time-to-threshold is
measurement (eval sampling noise, eval cadence, host speed) and how much is the RL run genuinely learning
at different speeds?

## 004_nokl_guardrail_h100

Seeds: 10 (10 crossed, 0 censored). Eval every ~25 steps on n = 2000; Wilson 95 % half-width at p = 0.10 is ±1.39 pp.

| quantity | mean ± sd | CV |
|---|---:|---:|
| time to threshold (s) | 958.1 ± 348.2 | 0.36 |
| steps to threshold | 390 ± 124 | 0.32 |
| inclusive s/step up to crossing | 2.428 ± 0.135 | 0.056 |

Variance decomposition (`CV² ≈ CV²_steps + CV²_speed`): steps-to-threshold explains 97 % of the time variance, host speed 3 %.

Median slope over the last eval interval: 3.70 pp / 100 steps = 0.82 pp / min. At that slope the ±1.39 pp eval sampling error alone moves the crossing time by ±103 s (median over seeds; per-seed column above), vs. an observed seed sd of 348 s.
Eval cadence: crossings are only observed every 65 s on average, so the clock quantisation adds up to that much (mean ~half) per seed.
Overshoot at the crossing eval: mean 0.60 pp, max 1.65 pp. 9/10 crossing evals have their Wilson 95 % interval straddling 0.10, i.e. the 'crossing' is not statistically distinguishable from being just below threshold.
Longest run of byte-identical consecutive eval pass rates per seed: [1, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 4, 1, 1, 2] — runs ≥ 3 are stalls where greedy outputs did not change for ≥ 50 steps.
